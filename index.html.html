The story behind the lens

Meet Bayo

A love of people, meaningful moments and visual storytelling, one photograph at a time.

Welcome to Instaby908 Photography, where the moments that matter become photographs to return to. I’m Bayo, the creative eye behind the camera, based in beautiful Victoria, British Columbia. My aim is simple: to make images that feel personal, honest and lasting.

My academic journey began in architecture, but along the way I found myself drawn to hospitality, tourism and the art of bringing people together. One of my early passion projects, Light Em Up in Abuja, gave me room to explore the energy of events and the connections formed when people gather to celebrate.

Moving to Canada gave me the space to spend more time behind the camera, refine my creative vision and turn that growing passion into something I could share. Away from the camera, I’m often travelling with my partner, discovering new foods, or dancing whenever the opportunity presents itself.

One of the most rewarding parts of my work is seeing clients return, grow familiar, and become something like family.
